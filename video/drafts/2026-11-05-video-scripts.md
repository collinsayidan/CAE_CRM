# CAE CRM videos: scripts and storyboards

Drafted 5 November 2026. Captions and music only, no voiceover.

## Ground rules

- Filmed on a local demo workspace with made-up UK businesses. No real customer data on screen.
- A small "Sample data" tag stays in one corner whenever figures are on screen.
- Only claims the homepage already makes. Price as live: £15 per user a month, 14 days free, no card needed.
- Captions are burned in, large enough to read on a phone with the sound off.
- Brand: royal blue (`#1e5bd6`), coral call-to-action, the app's own fonts, coral logo.
- Motion: slow push-ins on real screens, a soft highlighted cursor, callouts that point at the thing being described. Nothing flashy, and no fast cuts under one second.

## 1. Walkthrough, 60 seconds (16:9, 1920×1080)

| Time | Screen | Motion | Caption |

| 0–4s | Brand card | Logo draws in, tagline rises | **The CRM that already knows how UK business works.** |

| 12–20s | Web form, then the new contact | Form fills in, cut to the contact's timeline | Website enquiries arrive as leads. |

| 44–52s | Support ticket | Callout on the deadline countdown | Service deadlines that skip bank holidays. |
| 52–60s | End card | Price and call to action build in | **Every feature included. £15 per user a month.** 14 days free, no card needed. cae.collinalitics.co.uk |
