What it does

  • Answers questions about your contacts, companies, deals, tickets, quotes, invoices and tasks.
  • Explains how to do things in CAE CRM.
  • Drafts emails and notes for you to copy. It can't send anything.
  • Suggests tasks, notes and deal stage changes. Nothing changes until the person using it presses Confirm.
  • Adds helpers to everyday pages: Summarise and Brief me on contacts, companies and deals, Draft with AI for ticket replies, and a suggested category, priority and owner for new tickets, which a person chooses whether to apply.

Everyone in the workspace can use it, up to {{ limit }} questions a person a day. It sees the same records your team can see, but not admin areas such as billing, API keys or the audit trail.

What's shared, and with whom

The assistant runs on Claude, made by Anthropic. When someone asks a question, the question and the records the assistant looks up to answer it are sent to Anthropic, in the United States, to produce the answer.

  • Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.
  • Anthropic deletes it from its systems within 30 days, unless it's needed to investigate misuse or by law.
  • Conversations are kept in CAE CRM for {{ keep_days }} days, visible only to the person who had them, and they can delete them sooner.
  • Erasing a contact also deletes conversations that mention them.

Anthropic is listed as a sub-processor in our data processing agreement and privacy notice. Before turning this on, check it fits how you've told your own customers you use their data.
