Keep this address private: anything sent to it becomes a ticket. Set your own support address (for example support@yourbusiness.co.uk) to forward every email to it.

  • Microsoft 365 or Outlook: in Outlook on the web, open Settings, Mail, Forwarding, tick Enable forwarding, paste the address and choose Keep a copy. An admin may need to allow external forwarding in Microsoft 365 Defender.
  • Google Workspace or Gmail: open Settings, Forwarding and POP/IMAP, Add a forwarding address, paste the address and confirm it. The confirmation email arrives here as a ticket; open it to find the link.
  • Anywhere else: add a forwarding rule (or alias) in your email provider's settings that sends a copy of every message to the address.

Replies you send from a ticket use that ticket's own reply address, so customers' answers come back to it. Auto-replies and bounces are ignored.
