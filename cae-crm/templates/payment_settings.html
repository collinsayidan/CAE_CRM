Late payment interest

Under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, a business customer who pays late owes interest at 8% over the Bank of England reference rate, plus fixed compensation of £40, £70 or £100 depending on the size of the debt. Overdue invoices show what you can claim, and you choose whether to add it.
