This notice explains what personal data {{ legal_detail('LEGAL_COMPANY_NAME') }} ("we") collects through CAE CRM, why, and your rights. We're registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, number {{ legal_detail('LEGAL_ICO_NUMBER') }}.

Two different roles

  • We're the controller for data about the people who use CAE CRM (you and your team), about billing, and about visitors to this website. This notice covers that.
  • We're a processor for the customer and contact records that businesses keep in CAE CRM, including people who fill in a business's web form or sign one of its quotes. That business is the controller and decides how the data is used. If you're one of those people, contact the business directly. We'll help them respond. Our data processing agreement covers how we handle that data.

Who we share it with

We use these providers to run CAE CRM. They act on our instructions and must protect the data:

  • Vercel: hosting.
  • Supabase: database and file storage, in {{ legal_detail('LEGAL_DATA_REGION') }}.
  • Resend: sending email.
  • Stripe: payments and invoices for your subscription.
  • Sentry: error reports, if switched on.
  • Anthropic: the AI assistant, only if your workspace's admin turns it on. Anthropic receives the questions asked and the records the assistant looks up, in the United States. It doesn't use them to train its models, and deletes them within 30 days, unless it flags them for breaking its usage policy (then it can keep them for up to 2 years) or the law requires otherwise.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising. We only share it with anyone else if the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

Outside the UK

Some of these providers are based in, or use services in, the United States or the EU. Where data leaves the UK, it's protected by UK adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or the UK's International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. {{ to_confirm('which applies to each provider') }}

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile details: while you're on a team. When an admin removes you, they're deleted. Your name may stay on records you created or changed, such as notes and the audit trail, because those belong to the business.
  • Sign-in sessions: end after 12 hours. Password reset links expire after an hour.
  • Billing records: six years, as UK tax law requires.
  • Webhook delivery logs: 30 days.
  • AI assistant conversations: 30 days from the last message, unless you delete them sooner. Erasing a contact also deletes conversations that mention them. A count of questions asked and the amount of AI processing used, without their content, is kept for 13 months.
  • Workspace data: deleted within 30 days of the 30-day export window that follows a workspace closing (see the terms).
  • Backups and error reports: kept for a limited period, then overwritten. {{ to_confirm('backup and error report retention periods') }}

Your rights

You can ask us:

  • for a copy of your personal data;
  • to correct it;
  • to delete it;
  • to restrict how we use it;
  • to stop using it, where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • to send it to you or someone else in a reusable format.

You can change most profile details yourself under Profile and password. To use any other right, email {{ legal_detail('LEGAL_EMAIL') }}. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

Here are the main ways we protect your data:

  • It's encrypted on its way to and from the service.
  • Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes.
  • Admins must use two-factor sign-in.
  • Each business's data is kept apart from every other business's.

The data processing agreement lists our security measures in full.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we'll tell users by email or in the app before the change applies.
