# CAE CRM launch film: script and storyboard

Look: dark cinematic. Near-black stage (#05070d), blue light from the product (#1e5bd6), yellow (#ffc93c) and coral (#ff5f3d) only as accents. Plus Jakarta Sans, 800 weight, tight tracking. No voiceover: the words on screen carry the story, so it works muted on social.

Picture: app captured at 3x, rendered in 4K (3840×2160), lossless frames, high bitrate. 1080p copies for social.

Sound: your licensed track. Cuts land on its beats; the reveal lands on the drop.

## 30-second cut

Cold open (3s) → reveal (5s) → Sales (5s) → Money (6s) → Service (4s) → Mobile (3s) → offer (4s).

## 15-second vertical ad (9:16)

Hook "Sales, quotes, invoices, support. Four apps?" (2.5s) → "Meet CAE CRM." on a phone (2.5s) → three phone screens, one per beat (6s) → offer (4s).

## Facts used (all from the app as filmed)

- Web form enquiries arrive as leads; deals move by drag and drop.
- Quotes are signed online; invoices show VAT and take card payments, with reminders.
- Ticket service deadlines skip UK bank holidays.
- £15 per user a month; 14-day free trial; no card needed.
- All app data on screen is sample data and is labelled as such.

## Sound (added 5 Nov 2026)

- **Voice:** Imogen (ElevenLabs v4 via Higgsfield), files in `public/vo/line-01…10.mp3`.
- **Music:** ElevenLabs Music v2.5, "Take A", `public/music/launch-take-a.mp3`, 120 BPM. Each cut plays it from an offset so its drop (16s in the track) lands on the reveal, and splices to the track's ending on a whole bar so it resolves on the offer.
- **Effects:** made in code by `scripts/make-sfx.mjs` (whoosh, hit, riser, click, pop, tick, thud, chime, shimmer), so there is nothing to license.
- **Mix:** music at 0.8, ducked to 0.3 under the voice; peaks below -1 dB, no clipping.
